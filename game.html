How to read this board

  • Picks are shown in one flat list: Strong, then Lean, then Pass, and by EV per $100 within each tier. Rows with a Check or Thin market badge are always at the bottom.
  • The player name is on every row. Use the player chips to narrow the list. The list shows 100 picks at a time; use Show more to page through the rest.
  • If the modeler does not supply a tier or summary, the site falls back with these thresholds: Strong when EV per $100 is ≥ 5, edge is ≥ 4%, and confidence is not low; Lean when EV per $100 is > 0 and edge is ≥ 2%; Pass otherwise.
  • Site-side safety guards still apply when a tier is supplied: edge over 15% gets a Check badge and is never Strong; Kalshi rows with no volume and no open interest, or an ask−bid spread over 5 cents, get a Thin market badge and are never Strong or Lean. Rows that did not pass calibration have no tier and stay hidden by default.
