How to read this board

  • Every number is a model estimate, not a guarantee. This project does not place bets.
  • The list opens on Strong and Lean picks only, 100 at a time. Turn on Show Pass (or use a filter) to load the rest. Click a column header to re-sort. Filter by game, position, market, book, or minimum edge, and search for a player.
  • Suggested stake is quarter-Kelly, capped at 2% of bankroll, and is not betting advice. Rows whose model did not pass calibration are hidden unless you turn that toggle on.
  • If the modeler does not supply a tier or summary, the site falls back with these thresholds: Strong when EV per $100 is ≥ 5, edge is ≥ 4%, and confidence is not low; Lean when EV per $100 is > 0 and edge is ≥ 2%; Pass otherwise.
  • Site-side safety guards still apply when a tier is supplied: rows with edge over 15% get a Check badge and are never shown as Strong; Kalshi rows with no volume and no open interest, or an ask−bid spread over 5 cents, get a Thin market badge and are never Strong or Lean. Rows that did not pass calibration have no tier and stay hidden by default.
